What Are These Letters?

These letters are part of the new "tiered" system for my birthday and christmas lists. They work like ordinary letter grades you would use at a school, with one exception. "S" tier is the things I want most, "A" tier is the stuff that is a solid gift, "B" tier is the things I kinda want but don't really need that much, and "C" is the things that I think are cool, but should by no means get priority in your gift-choosing process.
