What Are These Letters?

These letters are part of the new "tiered" system for my birthday and christmas lists. They work like ordinary letter grades you would use at a school, with one exception. "S" tier is the things I want most, "A" tier is the stuff that is a solid gift, "B" tier is the things I kinda want but don't really need that much, and "C" is the things that I think are cool, but should by no means get priority in your gift-choosing process. In the tiers, items are organized in no particular order, so don't choose a gift based on its height in the tier it's in.

As a reminder, ONLY BUY THINGS FROM THE LINKS PROVIDED!

Special Rules for Gifts

If you get one of the DJ controllers, make sure no one else has gotten the same one or another one. I only need one DJ controller, and not every one listed here.
